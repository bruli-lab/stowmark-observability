Stowmark Observability is installed as release "{{ .Release.Name }}" in namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}".

Grafana
  kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} service/stowmark-grafana 3000:80
  Open http://localhost:3000 and sign in as "{{ .Values.grafana.adminUser }}" with:
  kubectl get secret stowmark-grafana --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    --output jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 --decode; echo

  The "Stowmark Overview" dashboard is in the "Stowmark" folder.

Sending telemetry from Stowmark
  Inside the cluster:
    OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=http://stowmark-otel-collector.{{ .Release.Namespace }}:4318
    OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_PROTOCOL=http/protobuf

  From outside the cluster:
    kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} service/stowmark-otel-collector 4317:4317 4318:4318
    OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT=http://localhost:4318

TraceQL metrics panels show data about 30 seconds after the first traces arrive.
